THE SOURCE PRINCIPLES

The slides define the strategy.

DCA determines buys; DVA determines sells. Seek long-term index growth with automatic full exits and same-session DCA reentry. Technical indicators, influencers, news, and predictions do not drive decisions. The slides supply the principles. User-confirmed rules retain full exits; this model permits same-session DCA reentry.
